# Privacy Policy: Platform Zero

_Last updated: 30 October 2026_

Platform Zero is a single-player game that runs entirely on your device.

## What we collect
Nothing. The game does not collect, store on a server, share or sell any personal data. There is no account, no sign-in and no analytics or advertising software in the app.

## What stays on your device
Your progress (items, tokens, reputation, settings) is saved in a file in the game's private storage on your phone. It never leaves the device. Uninstalling the game, or clearing its data in Android settings, deletes it.

## Permissions
The app asks only for permission to vibrate (for gentle haptic feedback). It does not request internet access, location, contacts, camera, microphone or storage.

## Children
Because no data is collected, the game contains nothing that tracks or identifies children or anyone else.

## Third parties
The game contains no third-party advertising, analytics or tracking libraries.

## Changes
If this ever changes, this page will be updated and the date above changed. Any change that involves collecting data would come with a new app version and a clear in-game notice.
