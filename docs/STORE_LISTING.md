## Full description (1556 chars)
You are the attendant of the Lost Property car on the Zero Line, a night train that never reaches its last stop. Things get left behind. People come looking. Your job is to match the two.

Search the cars for forgotten items: a red mitten, a pocket watch, a jar of something that rattles. Set each one on your bench and look closer with five hand tools. The Magnifier reads initials and station stamps, the Lamp shows colour and material, the Ear listens, the Nose sniffs out smells, and the Brush reveals wear.

Then the owners arrive. Some are calm, some are in a hurry, some are shy and some are grumpy, and you can read it in how they stand. Ask three questions, compare their answers with what the bench told you, and hand over the right item. Be careful: now and then an imposter turns up with one detail wrong, and politely refusing them is rewarded.

Earn tokens and reputation, hire porters, widen your bench and open new cars of the train: sleeper, observation, luggage. Each car has its own seats, passengers and secrets. Between stops the scenery drifts by, from forests and cities to snow, sea and desert nights.

Come back after a while and the train has kept going: new stops, new finds, new faces waiting at your counter. Once a night, a special case arrives for you alone.

There is no timer to beat, no way to fail and nothing is ever lost for good. No ads. No purchases. No account. Play for two minutes or an hour, in portrait or landscape.

Scraps of lore are tucked away in the cars, if you care to find out why the train never stops.
